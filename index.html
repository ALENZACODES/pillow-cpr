PillowCPR

Anyone can learn CPR
in 2 minutes — with a pillow.

Put a pillow on the floor, kneel in front of your webcam, and press. Pillow CPR watches your shoulders and wrists, counts every compression, and coaches your rate and form out loud — no course, no cost, no install.

Camera stays on your device. No video is recorded, uploaded, or stored — pose tracking runs entirely in your browser.

2–3× Immediate CPR can double or triple a cardiac arrest victim’s chance of survival.
100–120 Compressions per minute is the target rate — about two pushes every second.
~90% Of people who suffer cardiac arrest outside a hospital die — most never get CPR in time.

Source: American Heart Association, Hands-Only CPR fact sheet (heart.org).

How it works

1

Set up

Pillow on the floor. Kneel behind it, laptop in front of you, hands stacked in the centre of the pillow, shoulders over your hands.

2

Press to the coach

Pose tracking follows the up-and-down cycle of your wrists. The voice coach corrects you live: “faster,” “lock your elbows,” “let it come all the way up.”

3

Get your score

After 2 minutes: a score for rate, consistency and arm form — and a shareable card that says you practiced.

Honest limits

  • This is a practice tool, not a medical device and not a certification. Take a certified course — the American Heart Association and the Heart and Stroke Foundation run them everywhere.
  • A webcam measures rate and arm form reliably. True compression depth can’t be measured from a flat camera image, so Pillow CPR shows press depth as a rough estimate only.
  • In a real emergency: call 911 first, then push hard and fast in the centre of the chest. Emergency mode is built for exactly that moment.

Get in position

Starting your camera…
  1. Pillow flat on the floor in front of the laptop.
  2. Kneel behind the pillow, hands stacked in its centre.
  3. Shoulders directly over your hands, arms straight.
  4. You should see your shoulders and hands in the frame.
  • Shoulders in view
  • Hands / wrists in view
  • Far enough back — upper body fits

Nothing leaves your device: the pose model runs in this tab and frames are never sent anywhere.
